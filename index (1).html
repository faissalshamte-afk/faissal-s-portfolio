// 01 — ABOUT

Built around machines, wired for people.

I'm Faissal Khamis, currently pursuing a Mechatronics diploma at MUST (Mbeya University of Science and Technology). I got here through Tanga Technical Secondary School, where the mix of hands-on drawing and technical thinking first pulled me toward engineering.

I'm passionate about machines and smart systems — how they're built, how they think, and how they solve real problems. I'm especially drawn to airplanes and everything about how they're engineered to defy gravity with precision.

My goal is simple but big: become a good engineer at a respected engineering company, and use that position to help people fulfill their dreams — the way good engineering has always quietly made other people's ambitions possible.
